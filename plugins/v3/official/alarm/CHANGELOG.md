## 1.1.0

设置补完整，岛上默认只在响铃时出现。

- 已有闹钟可以编辑了：点岛上的闹钟或设置里的「编辑」，改时间、备注、重复和铃声，也能删除。
- 岛上的添加表单只留时间、备注和「添加」；重复和铃声折成一行，点开再改。试听挪进设置。
- 新的响铃设置：音量（70%）、渐强（关）、贪睡时长（5 分钟）、响铃多久自动停（30 秒）、单次闹钟响过后保留并关掉或自动删除。
- 错过的闹钟（关机、睡眠）回来后弹一条提醒，可以在设置里关掉。
- 新的显示设置：时间格式（跟随系统 / 24 小时 / 12 小时）、一周从哪天开始。
- 收起条新装默认只在响铃时显示，可以改成常驻显示下一次闹钟；点收起条打开闹钟页（需要浮屿支持）。
- 贪睡中的闹钟浮屿重启后不再丢失。
- 命令「停止闹钟响铃」接上了，新增命令「闹钟贪睡」，都能在插件详情里设快捷键。
- 岛的页头按钮有了中英文标题。
- 原来的闹钟和默认铃声照旧保留；原来开着「在收起条显示下一次闹钟」的，收起条保持常驻；新设置取默认值。

Fuller settings; by default the alarm shows on the island only while it rings.

- Existing alarms can be edited: click one on the island, or "Edit" in the settings, to change its time, label, days and sound, or delete it.
- The island's add form keeps just the time, the label and "Add"; days and sound fold into one line you click to change. "Test sound" moved to the settings.
- New ringing settings: volume (70%), start quiet and get louder (off), snooze length (5 minutes), stop ringing after (30 seconds), and keep or delete one-time alarms after they ring.
- Alarms missed while the computer was off or asleep show up as a notification when it is back; you can turn this off.
- New display settings: time format (system, 24-hour, 12-hour) and first day of the week.
- On new installs the collapsed bar shows only while an alarm rings; you can make it always show the next alarm. Clicking it opens the Alarm page (when Fuyu supports it).
- A snoozed alarm is no longer lost when Fuyu restarts.
- The "Dismiss ringing alarm" command now works, and there is a new "Snooze ringing alarm" command; both can get shortcuts in the plugin's details.
- The island header button has a title in Chinese and English.
- Your alarms and default sound carry over; if "Show next alarm on collapsed bar" was on, the bar keeps showing it; new settings start at their defaults.
