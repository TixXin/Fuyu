# 更新记录 · Changelog

## 1.1.0

设置补完整，岛上的页面只留搜索框和结果。

- 搜索框为空时默认只显示最近使用的 8 个应用，不再把全部应用列出来；设置里可以改成显示全部，或者改显示几个。
- 岛上的页面去掉了文件夹名、「最近 · N 次」徽标、底部的按键提示和「重新扫描」按钮。文件夹和打开次数可以在设置里打开；名字相同的两个应用照样会显示文件夹，好分清。
- 新设置：排除的应用、额外扫描的文件夹（Windows 上还能找到里面的 .exe）、自动重新扫描的间隔（5 分钟）、是否记住打开过的应用、最多记住几个（200 个），「重新扫描」挪到了设置里。
- 新设置「打开应用后收起岛」（开）；新命令「打开应用启动台」，可以在插件详情里设快捷键。这两项需要较新的浮屿，旧版本里不出现。
- 命令「重新扫描应用」接上了，在插件详情里能运行、能设快捷键。
- 名字相同、位置不同的两个应用不再被合成一个。
- 名字统一为「应用启动台 / App launcher」，岛的页头按钮有了中英文标题；补了截图和新的说明。
- 原来的最近启动记录照旧保留；新设置取默认值。
- 图标换成和浮屿设置一致的风格：浅底色加同色线条。
- 市场页的截图换成了新的，更清楚。

Fuller settings; the island page keeps just the search box and the results.

- With an empty search the page now shows only your 8 most recent apps instead of every app; in the settings you can show all apps instead, or change how many recent ones appear.
- The island page drops the folder name, the "Recent · Nx" badge, the key hints at the bottom and the "Rescan" button. Folders and launch counts can be turned on in the settings; two apps with the same name always show their folders so you can tell them apart.
- New settings: excluded apps, extra folders to scan (on Windows they can also hold .exe files), how often to rescan (every 5 minutes), whether to remember the apps you open, and how many to remember (200). "Rescan" moved into the settings.
- New setting "Collapse the island after opening an app" (on) and a new "Open App launcher" command you can give a shortcut in the plugin's details. Both need a newer Fuyu and are hidden on older versions.
- The "Rescan apps" command now works: run it or give it a shortcut in the plugin's details.
- Two apps with the same name in different places are no longer merged into one.
- The name is now "应用启动台 / App launcher" everywhere, the island header button has a title in Chinese and English, and there are screenshots and a new description.
- Your recent history carries over; new settings start at their defaults.
- New icon in the same style as Fuyu's settings: a tinted tile with a line glyph in the same color.
- New, sharper screenshot on the market page.
