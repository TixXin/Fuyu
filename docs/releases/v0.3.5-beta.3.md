## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.5 的第三个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **浅色和深色（新）**：设置 → 主题 最上面可以选深色、浅色或跟随系统。深色和浅色各有一套背景、文字和亮点颜色，都可以单独调（「调整」里选要改哪一套）。浅色的岛会把文字、分隔线和按钮底色都换成深色，消息、邮件、歌词、插件页面、翻译等页面都跟着变。以前的设置和主题文件会自动归到看起来对应的那一套，升级后外观不变。
- **收起时保持深色**：浅色的岛可以在收起时保持深色，和屏幕顶部的黑边、刘海融在一起，展开时再渐变成浅色。新安装默认打开，老用户默认关闭。
- **页面风格统一**：待办、剪贴板、文件中转三个页面和 Agent 页一样直接放在岛上，不再套卡片。待办页顶部有分类小标签和今天的完成进度，保存等操作收进「⋯」，已完成的待办折叠在下面；剪贴板和文件中转每行两行显示，操作按钮悬停才出现，点一行就复制。
- **闲置自动收起更聪明**：设置 → 行为 → 闲置时收起到边缘 新增三个开关：有正在进行的待办时、有 AI 会话在等你批准或回答时（这两个默认开），以及正在放音乐时（默认关），岛不会自动收起。
- **更新设置更完整**：设置 → 关于 可以选多久检查一次更新（1 / 6 / 12 / 24 小时），以及发现新版本时只在岛上提示，还是也发一条系统通知。页面底部写着上次检查成功的时间；新增「诊断信息」，可以一键复制版本、系统、通道和更新状态，方便反馈问题。
- **修复**：
  - 剪贴板历史在文件被其他程序短暂占用时不再丢失写入。
  - 修复每次启动时岛屿第一次调整窗口大小失败的问题。

## About this release

This is the third beta of 0.3.5. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **Light and dark (new)**: Settings → Theme now starts with Dark, Light or System. Dark and light each have their own background, text and accent colours, adjustable separately (pick which set under Adjusting). A light island turns its text, dividers and button fills dark, on the messages, mail, lyrics, plugin, translation and other pages too. Earlier settings and theme files go into the set they look like, so nothing looks different after the update.
- **Stay dark when collapsed**: a light island can stay dark while collapsed, blending into the screen's black top edge and notch, and fade to light as it opens. On for new installs, off for existing ones.
- **One look for the pages**: the todo, clipboard and file shelf pages sit straight on the island like the Agent page, without a card. The todo page gets category chips and today's progress at the top, saving and similar actions under "⋯", and done todos folded below; clipboard and shelf rows take two lines, show their buttons on hover, and copy on a click.
- **Smarter idle tuck**: Settings → Behavior → Tuck to the edge when idle gains three switches that keep the island out: a todo in progress and an AI session waiting for your approval or answer (both on), and music playing (off).
- **Fuller update settings**: Settings → About picks how often to check for updates (every 1, 6, 12 or 24 hours) and whether a new version is announced on the island only or with a system notification as well. The footer shows the last successful check, and a new Diagnostics row copies the version, system, channel and update state for a bug report.
- **Fixes**:
  - Clipboard history no longer loses a write while another program briefly holds its file.
  - The island's first window sizing no longer fails on every start.
