## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.8 的第二个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **截图时按住 `M` 测量**：截图界面按住 `M`，指针所在的色块显示宽 × 高和到边缘的辅助线；按住 `M` 拖动是一把尺子，显示距离和横纵偏移。松开就消失，不影响选区和标注。
- **截图打开更快**：按下快捷键后压暗的截图界面马上出现，画面在后面截。
- **Agent 页多了三种会话**：WorkBuddy、Antigravity 和 DeepSeek Harness 的会话也会列出来，带各自的图标；WorkBuddy 等你授权时显示「等你」。会话时间显示在归档按钮的位置，鼠标移上去才换成归档按钮。
- **剪贴板**：
  - 按住空格预览一条，松开就关；轻按一下仍是打开，再按一次关。
  - 复制的图片文件显示缩略图、尺寸和大小，其他文件按类型显示图标并写出所在文件夹。
  - 时间放在每行右边；「链接」改叫「网址」，「片段」改叫「常用语」；开了「读取链接标题和图标」时网址显示网站图标；`Ctrl+1`–`9` 选中并预览那一行，不粘贴。
  - 截图时剪贴板窗口开着，浮屿自己的截图会把它截进去，截完它还开着。
- **录快捷键时能录已在用的组合**：设置里录快捷键时，按浮屿自己已经注册的快捷键不再触发功能，而是录进去。
- **修复**：
  - 剪贴板窗口正好显示完整的九行，鼠标停在最后一行时列表不再自己往下滚。
  - `Ctrl+Tab`、`Alt+数字` 切换页面的顺序和顶栏图标一致。
  - 自动弹出的 Agent 提醒卡片和新消息面板，鼠标停在上面时最多保持 30 秒。
  - 启动失败的那一次不会再用旧的待办覆盖新的。
  - 待办行更紧凑。
  - 各个窗口打开更快。

## About this release

This is the second beta of 0.3.8. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **Hold `M` to measure in screenshots**: holding `M` on the screenshot view shows the width × height of the block under the pointer with lines to its edges; drag while holding `M` for a ruler with the distance and horizontal / vertical offsets. Letting go clears it and leaves the selection and annotations alone.
- **Screenshots open faster**: the dimmed screenshot view appears as soon as you press the shortcut, and the picture is captured behind it.
- **Three more kinds of sessions on the Agent page**: WorkBuddy, Antigravity and DeepSeek Harness sessions are listed with their own icons, and a WorkBuddy session asking for permission shows as waiting. A session's time now sits where the archive button is and turns into the button on hover.
- **Clipboard**:
  - Hold Space to peek at an entry and let go to close it; a quick tap still opens it until you press again.
  - Copied image files show a thumbnail with their size; other files get an icon for their kind and show their folder.
  - The time sits on the right of each row; with "Read link titles and icons" on, links show the site's icon; `Ctrl+1`–`9` selects and previews that row without pasting.
  - Fuyu's own screenshots include the clipboard window when it is open, and it stays open afterwards.
- **Record shortcuts Fuyu already uses**: while recording a shortcut in Settings, pressing one of Fuyu's own shortcuts records it instead of running it.
- **Fixes**:
  - The clipboard window shows exactly nine whole rows, and resting on the last one no longer scrolls the list by itself.
  - `Ctrl+Tab` and `Alt+digit` go through pages in the same order as the header icons.
  - Agent alert cards and the new-messages panel that open by themselves stay at most 30 seconds while hovered.
  - A start that fails no longer overwrites newer todos with older ones.
  - Todo rows are more compact.
  - Windows open faster.
