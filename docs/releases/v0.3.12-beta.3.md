这是 0.3.12 的第三个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。已经装了浮屿的，在 设置 → 关于 里更新就行；手动安装时 Windows 直接覆盖安装，Mac 把新的 Fuyu 拖进「应用程序」替换旧的。待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。Mac 版第一次打开要在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」，步骤见[在 Mac 上安装浮屿](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/blob/main/docs/features/macos-install.md)。

## 更新内容

### 岛的开合和悬停

- **新的「弹开」动画**：默认的开合动画换成「弹开」，照 Nook 逐帧测量的弹簧复刻，宽度冲出去再回来，更顺。新增「弹动幅度」（轻微 / 常规 / 弹动），调展开和碰到岛时回弹多少。新装默认用它，已经选过别的不变。
- **悬停效果可以选**：设置 → 行为 里可以选鼠标靠近收起的岛时怎么反应，默认「碰到弹一下」，也可以关掉。
- **Fuyu 页小组件出场动效**：展开面板时小组件依次出场，共十一种，默认每次换一种，数字、环形进度和格子会跟着动起来。在 设置 → 行为 → 展开 里可以固定一种或关掉。
- **Fuyu 页标签可以换成水母图标**（新，默认关闭）：设置 → 行为 → 展开 →「Fuyu 页标签用图标」。
- （Mac）**更顺**：在 120Hz 屏幕的 Mac 上，展开收起跟上屏幕刷新率，刚收起又打开时不再卡一下。

### 改尺寸

- **新的改尺寸方式**：岛和面板的边上不再一碰就出提示线，改成：面板底部的拉环，边缘停一下再拖，下角旋钮整档切换（紧凑 / 默认 / 宽大），右键「调整大小…」可以用方向键微调、Esc 撤销。收起的岛拖两端的小拉环改宽度。拖旋钮时比当前大的几档虚线框也能完整看到。

### Windows 11 任务栏

- （Windows）**岛可以放进任务栏**：Windows 11 上 设置 → 外观 → 位置 →「吸附到」新增「任务栏」，收起的岛放进任务栏，展开时面板从任务栏上方弹出。按住岛左边的竖线可以沿任务栏拖到想要的位置。收起时默认透明，像任务栏按钮一样，也可以改成跟岛的主题一样。任务栏放不下、自动隐藏或有全屏程序时，退回贴在屏幕底边。

### 其他

- **按钮提示统一 0.4 秒**：所有窗口的按钮名称提示都是停 0.4 秒后出现。
- **剪贴板窗口可以不预先准备**：设置 → 剪贴板 新增「启动时预先准备剪贴板窗口」。打开时第一次按快捷键马上出来，代价是一直多占约 40 MB；关掉时第一次打开稍慢、隐藏 10 分钟后释放。Windows 上默认打开，Mac 上默认关闭。
- （Mac）**省内存**：岛不再越用越多地占着应用图标和系统对象（原来可能多占几百 MB）；收起时少占约 45 MB 图形内存；识别图片文字后不再一直多占五十多 MB。
- （Mac）**截图按 Esc 直接退出**，不用先点一下选区。

### 修复

- （Mac）岛闲置一会儿后第一次展开没有动画、直接弹开。
- 岛偶尔在展开、收起时卡一下（摄像头麦克风检测不再占用主线程）；悬停、开合时整页重新计算样式导致的卡顿也减轻了。
- 面板开合时岛左右晃一下；悬停时收起的岛偶尔左右抖。
- 鼠标停在岛上时点开，开合动画被跳过。
- 改尺寸的手柄碰得到却拖不动、指针不变；折叠岛改宽度时端头并排出现两条竖线。
- 快速拖面板边缘、松手时鼠标刚好在外面，面板会收起。

## About this release

**Download**: [Windows](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/releases/download/v0.3.12-beta.3/Fuyu_0.3.12-beta.3_x64-setup.exe) · [Mac (Apple silicon)](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/releases/download/v0.3.12-beta.3/Fuyu_0.3.12-beta.3_aarch64.dmg)

This is the third beta of 0.3.12. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. If Fuyu is installed, update it from Settings → About. To install by hand, run the Windows installer over your current version, or drag the new Fuyu into Applications on a Mac to replace the old one. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept. The first time you open the Mac app, click "Open Anyway" in System Settings → Privacy & Security ([steps, in Chinese](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/blob/main/docs/features/macos-install.md)).

## What's New

### Opening, closing and hover

- **A new Pop motion**: the default open and close motion is now Pop, rebuilt from springs measured frame by frame in Nook, with the width overshooting and settling back. A new Bounce strength setting (Subtle / Regular / Bouncy) sets how far the island overshoots when it opens and when the pointer touches it. New installs use it; a motion you picked before is kept.
- **Choose the hover effect**: Settings → Behaviour lets you pick how the collapsed island reacts when the pointer comes near. The default is Bounce on touch, and it can be turned off.
- **Widgets animate in on the Fuyu page**: when the panel opens, the widgets come in one after another, with eleven entrances shuffled by default and numbers, rings and grids that come alive. Pick one or turn it off under Settings → Behaviour → Opening.
- **A jellyfish icon for the Fuyu tab** (new, off by default): Settings → Behaviour → Opening → "Icon for the Fuyu tab".
- (Mac) **Smoother**: on Macs with 120 Hz screens, opening and closing run at the screen's refresh rate, and reopening right after closing no longer stutters.

### Resizing

- **A new way to resize**: the edges of the island and panel no longer show resize lines as soon as you touch them. Instead there is a grabber at the bottom of the panel, edges that arm after a short rest, corner knobs that switch whole presets (Compact / Default / Large), and "Resize…" in the right-click menu with arrow-key nudging and Esc to undo. On the collapsed island, drag the small caps at either end to change its width. While you drag a knob, the outlines of the larger presets show in full.

### Windows 11 taskbar

- (Windows) **Put the island in the taskbar**: on Windows 11, Settings → Appearance → Position → Dock to has a new Taskbar option. The collapsed island sits in the taskbar and the panel opens just above it. Press the line on the island's left to drag it along the taskbar. While collapsed it is see-through like a taskbar button by default, or it can keep the island theme. With no room, an auto-hidden taskbar or a fullscreen app, it falls back to the bottom edge of the screen.

### Other

- **Tooltips after 0.4s everywhere**: button name tooltips appear after 0.4 seconds in every window.
- **The clipboard window no longer has to be prepared**: Settings → Clipboard has a new "Prepare the clipboard window at startup". On, the first shortcut press opens it instantly but it keeps about 40 MB; off, the first open is a little slower and the window is freed after 10 minutes hidden. On by default on Windows, off on a Mac.
- (Mac) **Less memory**: the island no longer keeps piling up app icons and system objects (which could add up to hundreds of MB), uses about 45 MB less graphics memory while collapsed, and no longer holds 50+ MB after reading text from an image.
- (Mac) **Esc cancels a screenshot right away**, without clicking the overlay first.

### Fixes

- (Mac) The island sometimes opened without its animation the first time after sitting idle.
- Occasional stutters when the island opens or closes are gone (the camera and microphone check no longer blocks the main thread), and restyling the whole page on every hover and open frame no longer slows them down.
- The island shook sideways when its panel opened or closed, and the collapsed island sometimes jittered on hover.
- Clicking the island while hovering it skipped the open animation.
- Resize handles lit up without changing the cursor or responding to a drag, and the collapsed island's end showed two lines side by side while resizing.
- Dragging the panel edge quickly and letting go just outside collapsed the panel.
