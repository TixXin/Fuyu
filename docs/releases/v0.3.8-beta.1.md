## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.8 的第一个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **长截图**：截图工具栏里新增长截图按钮（也可以按 `Ctrl+Shift+5`，数字行和小键盘都行）。框选要滚动的区域后，截图界面、压暗和辅助线都留着，框里显示实时页面，旁边出现长截图的控制条：已拼的高度和屏数、最新一截的预览、到 2 万像素上限的进度。长截图不会自己开始，按空格或点 ▶ 才往下滚，也可以自己在框里滚，照样拼进去；滚到底会自动停下。空格暂停 / 继续，`Enter` 完成并复制，`Ctrl+S` 保存，`Esc` 取消。顶部、底部固定的栏只保留一份。
- **截图自动打码**：截图工具栏里的「自动打码」会识别选区里的邮箱、手机号、IP 地址和各类密钥，盖上马赛克；点一处可以取消，撤销会一处一处撤回。
- **截图打开更快**：按下截图快捷键后，截图界面马上出现，不用再等画面传完。
- **岛可以吸附到屏幕左右两边**：把收起的岛拖到屏幕最左或最右边松手，它会收成一个小把手，上面仍显示 Agent 状态和未读；点开时面板从这一侧展开。把把手拖离边缘就变回普通的岛。
- **邮箱一键全部已读**：消息 → 邮箱的工具栏多了一个按钮，把当前邮箱里的未读全部标为已读（邮箱里也会变成已读，不删邮件）。新消息弹出面板里的「清除提醒」现在也会把列出的邮件标为已读；微信的清除按钮也会同时去掉微信的预览。
- **图标按钮显示名字**：鼠标停在岛上只有图标的按钮上一会儿，就会显示它是做什么的；用键盘移到按钮上时马上显示。
- **第一次的小提示**：第一次出现 Agent 会话、第一次收到消息、第一次贴边收起时，收起条上会轻轻提示一句，一天最多一条，关掉后不再出现。
- **新的「关于」页**：设置 → 关于 顶部是一张显示版本、通道和更新状态的卡片，链接排成方块，「帮助」和「重置与退出」各自成组。
- **插件页更好找**：设置里已安装的插件可以搜索，并按「全部」「运行中」「有更新」「需要处理」筛选；每个插件的页面顶部有图标、名字和简介；插件市场顶部显示索引状态和已安装、可更新的数量。
- **新手引导更简单**：外观那一幕只问深色还是浅色和主题色；选用途时卡片列出会保留哪些功能、关掉了哪些，全部取消就恢复原样；从引导里点去设置会直接结束引导；从「关于」重新打开引导时，不再把桌面上的窗口都最小化。
- **插件市场：天气 1.0.0**：岛上的天气页显示当前天气、未来 24 小时和 7 天，可以在收起条上显示，下雨前一小时提醒。要先在插件设置里填城市才会联网，不读取系统定位。
- **修复**：
  - 不贴顶的岛上方两个角现在和下方一样是圆角。

## About this release

This is the first beta of 0.3.8. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **Long screenshots**: the screenshot toolbar has a long screenshot button (or press `Ctrl+Shift+5`, number row or numpad). After you pick the area that scrolls, the screenshot view stays up with its dimming and guides, the live page shows inside the frame, and a control bar appears beside it with the height and screens so far, a preview of the newest part and a meter to the 20,000 px limit. It doesn't start on its own: press Space or ▶ to scroll down, or scroll inside the frame yourself and that gets stitched too. It stops by itself at the end of the page. Space pauses and resumes, `Enter` finishes and copies, `Ctrl+S` saves, `Esc` cancels. Headers and footers that stay put are kept only once.
- **Auto-redact in screenshots**: Auto-redact on the screenshot toolbar finds emails, phone numbers, IP addresses and keys in the selection and covers them with a mosaic. Click one to remove it; undo takes them back one at a time.
- **Screenshots open faster**: the screenshot view now appears as soon as you press the shortcut instead of waiting for the picture to arrive.
- **Dock the island to the left or right edge**: drop the collapsed island at the far left or right of a screen and it folds into a small handle that still shows the agent status and unread dot; open it and the panel slides out from that side. Drag the handle away to get the normal island back.
- **Mark a whole mailbox read**: Messages → Mail has a new toolbar button that marks everything unread in the current mailbox as read, in the mailbox itself too (nothing is deleted). Clear in the new-messages popup now also marks the mails it lists as read, and the WeChat clear button also removes the WeChat preview.
- **Names for icon buttons**: rest the pointer on an icon-only button on the island and its name appears after a moment; with the keyboard it shows at once.
- **First-time hints**: the first time an agent session appears, a message arrives or the island tucks away, the collapsed bar shows a short hint, at most one a day, and never again once dismissed.
- **A new About page**: Settings → About opens with a card showing the version, channel and update status, the links are tiles, and Help and Reset and quit are their own groups.
- **Easier to find plugins**: installed plugins in Settings can be searched and filtered by All, Running, Updates and Needs attention; each plugin's page opens with its icon, name and description; the plugin market shows the index status and how many are installed or have updates.
- **A simpler first-run guide**: the look step only asks for dark or light and the accent colour; the use cards list which features are kept and which were turned off, and undoing every choice restores them; links to Settings end the guide; reopening it from About no longer minimizes every window on the desktop.
- **In the plugin market: Weather 1.0.0**: a Weather page on the island with the current weather, the next 24 hours and the next 7 days, an optional item on the collapsed bar and a heads-up an hour before rain. It goes online only after you type a city in its settings and never reads your location.
- **Fixes**:
  - An island that is not attached to the top edge now has rounded top corners like its bottom ones.
