## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.5 的第二个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **Alt 拖动与缩放**：移动岛屿改为按住 Alt 拖动。展开后，按住 Alt 拖动左右边缘调整宽度、底边调整高度、底角同时调整宽高；按住 Alt 或缩放时，面板不会因鼠标移出或失去焦点而收起。
- **各页面统一尺寸**：待办、音乐、日历、剪贴板、Agent、文件中转、消息、翻译和插件页面都使用「外观」里的面板宽度与高度，内容多时在内部滚动。宽度可设为 480–800，高度为 360–640（默认 560 × 490）；剪贴板和 Agent 的展开条数设置不再需要。自动弹出的消息和 Agent 提醒、请求卡片仍按内容适配。
- **设置更好找**：新增「行为」和「主题」页面；展开、收起和各类提醒移到「行为」，风格、颜色、圆角及已保存主题移到「主题」，「外观」保留尺寸和显示内容。
- **主题保留你的布局**：应用或导入主题只改变视觉样式，不再改动面板尺寸和显示选项；旧主题文件中的这些布局设置也会忽略。「重置主题」和「重置外观」分别恢复对应设置。
- **主题市场（新）**：在「主题」旁的「主题市场」预览主题、直接应用，或保存到自己的主题列表；应用市场主题同样保留你的布局。
- **收起栏歌词逐字高亮**：歌词随演唱进度点亮，长句可跟随当前歌词滚动；在 设置 → 音乐 → 岛上歌词 中调整高亮颜色、未唱部分亮度、发光效果和填充方式。没有逐字时间的歌词可选择按行时间平滑填充。
- **待办统一岛屿样式**：待办页面与岛屿其他页面保持一致，移除纸张样式及其专属颜色设置，待办和归档数据保留。
- **歌词同步修复**：修复网易云音乐等不提供播放位置的播放器在暂停、继续播放后，歌词跳回开头或时间偏移的问题。
- **开源许可说明**：应用从 0.3.5 起采用 GPL-3.0-or-later；插件 SDK、脚手架、PowerShell 模块和示例仍采用 MIT。

## About this release

This is the second beta of 0.3.5. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **Alt to move and resize**: hold Alt and drag to move the island. With the panel open, Alt-drag a side edge to change its width, the bottom edge to change its height, or a bottom corner to change both. Holding Alt or resizing keeps the panel open when the pointer leaves or focus changes.
- **Consistent page sizes**: todos, music, calendar, clipboard, Agent, file shelf, messages, translation and plugin pages now use the panel width and height from Appearance, with scrolling inside. Width ranges from 480–800 and height from 360–640 (default 560 × 490). The clipboard and Agent settings for the number of entries shown when open are no longer needed. Automatic message and Agent alert/request cards still fit their content.
- **Settings are easier to find**: new Behavior and Theme pages group opening, tucking and alerts under Behavior, and styles, colors, corners and saved themes under Theme. Appearance keeps sizes and displayed content.
- **Themes keep your layout**: applying or importing a theme changes its visual style without changing panel sizes or display options. Layout values in older theme files are ignored too. Reset theme and Reset appearance restore their own settings separately.
- **Theme market (new)**: preview themes in Theme market beside Theme, apply them directly, or save them to your own theme list. Market themes keep your layout too.
- **Karaoke lyrics on the collapsed bar**: lyrics light up as they are sung, and long lines can scroll to keep the current words visible. Settings → Music → Lyrics on the island offers highlight colors, unsung brightness, glow and fill styles. Lyrics without word timings can fill smoothly using line timings.
- **A consistent todo page**: todos now use the same island style as the other pages. The paper style and its dedicated color setting have been removed; todos and archives are kept.
- **Lyrics stay in sync**: fixed lyrics jumping back to the start or drifting after pause and play with players that do not report playback position, such as NetEase Cloud Music.
- **Open-source licensing**: the app uses GPL-3.0-or-later from 0.3.5 onward. The plugin SDK, scaffold, PowerShell module and examples remain MIT.
