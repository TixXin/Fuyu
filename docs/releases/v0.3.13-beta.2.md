## 更新内容

- **Fuyu 面板按大小自动排版**：拖窄、拖高面板时，组件会重新排成适合的几列几行（窄而高的面板变成 3、4 列多行），字号基本不变，不再整体缩小、留一大片空白；分页时不再露出下一页的一截。
- **Fuyu 面板的组件都能用了**：
  - 倒数日、习惯、世界时钟、日出日落、喝水目标可以在组件里直接改；番茄钟能设时长，走完会在岛上提醒，面板关着也照样结算。
  - 计时器一键开 5 / 15 / 25 分钟倒计时，待办组件能直接添加，音乐组件加了上一首、能点开音乐页。
  - 月历能点日子看当天日程、翻月、跳到日历页；下一个会议组件能直接加入会议。
  - Agent 会话点一下切到对应窗口；用量组件没数据时说明原因、能直接去设置，有数据时显示多久后重置。
  - 文件中转组件能拖出文件、双击打开、在文件夹中显示；剪贴板、截图、翻译组件点了会提示「已复制」，并能打开对应的完整页面。
  - 快捷启动能加自己的应用、删除和排序（鼠标移上去点编辑，或右键）；亮度条能拖动调节屏幕亮度，调不了的屏幕会说明原因。
  - 未读消息组件的大数字把邮件也算进去，微信显示为「有新消息」而不是 1。
- **岛屿右键菜单换成托盘卡片的样式**：岛吸附在任务栏上时菜单不再被岛挡住。
- **修复**：
  - 面板组件复制、打开成功后仍提示失败。
  - 日历一直停在之前点过的日子；年进度的剩余天数在夏令时地区多一天。
  - 调过岛屿大小后，新消息面板被截掉一截、页签高亮错位，Agent 列表重排和通知滚动条拖动不跟手。
  - 开着系统代理时，本机运行的翻译模型（如 Ollama）也走代理而连不上；现在直连，没启动时如实提示。

## About this release

**Download**: [Windows](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/releases/download/v0.3.13-beta.2/Fuyu_0.3.13-beta.2_x64-setup.exe) · [Mac (Apple silicon)](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/releases/download/v0.3.13-beta.2/Fuyu_0.3.13-beta.2_aarch64.dmg)

This is the second beta of 0.3.13. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. If Fuyu is installed, update it from Settings → About. To install by hand, run the Windows installer over your current version, or drag the new Fuyu into Applications on a Mac to replace the old one. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept. The first time you open the Mac app, click "Open Anyway" in System Settings → Privacy & Security ([steps, in Chinese](https://github.com/TixXin/fuyu/blob/main/docs/features/macos-install.md)).

## What's New

- **The Fuyu panel lays itself out to fit**: resizing the panel rearranges its widgets into columns and rows that fit (a tall narrow panel gets 3 or 4 columns and more rows) at nearly the same size, instead of shrinking everything and leaving empty space. Paged panels no longer show a slice of the next page.
- **Every Fuyu panel widget now does something**:
  - Countdowns, habits, world clocks, sunrise and the water goal can be edited in place; the pomodoro length can be set, and the island alerts when it ends, even with the panel closed.
  - The timer starts 5 / 15 / 25-minute countdowns in one click, the todos widget adds tasks inline, and the music widget gains previous-track and opens the music page.
  - The month calendar lets you pick a day, switch months and jump to the calendar; the next-meeting widget joins the meeting directly.
  - Agent sessions jump to their window when clicked; usage widgets explain missing data with a Set up button and show when the limit resets.
  - The file shelf widget drags files out, opens them and shows them in their folder; clipboard, screenshot and translate widgets confirm copies and open their full pages.
  - Quick launch holds your own apps, which you can remove and reorder (hover and click edit, or right-click); the brightness bar changes the screen brightness, and explains when a screen cannot be set.
  - The unread widget's big number now includes mail, and WeChat shows as "new" instead of a count of 1.
- **The island's right-click menu matches the tray card**, and no longer hides behind the island when it is docked on the taskbar.
- **Fixes**:
  - Panel widgets showed "failed" after a successful copy or open.
  - The calendar reopened on a previously clicked day; the year widget's days left was one too high in DST time zones.
  - After changing the island size, the new-message panel was cut short, the tab highlight was misplaced, and the agent list reorder and notice scrollbar lagged the pointer.
  - With a system proxy on, a translation model on this machine (such as Ollama) went through the proxy and failed; it is now reached directly, and a stopped one is reported as unreachable.
