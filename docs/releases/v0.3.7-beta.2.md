## 关于这个版本

这是 0.3.7 的第二个测试版，只推送给在 设置 → 关于 → 版本 里选择了「测试版」通道的用户，以及到 Release 页面手动下载安装的用户；稳定版用户不会收到。选测试版的用户正式版本发布时也照常收到；切回稳定版不会降级。直接覆盖安装即可，待办、笔记、设置和已连接的邮箱都会保留。

## 更新内容

- **截图能做更多事**：复制时同时放入图片、PNG 和文件，聊天软件、画图和终端都能直接粘贴；`Ctrl+S` 或工具栏保存到「图片\浮屿截图」，按时间命名；按 `T` 把选区钉在屏幕上原来的位置，可以拖动、滚轮缩放、`Ctrl` + 滚轮调透明度，右键菜单可复制、保存、恢复大小、鼠标穿透、全部隐藏或关闭，在钉图或岛上再按 `T` 隐藏或显示所有钉图。新快捷键 `Ctrl+Shift+2`：选好区域或窗口就直接复制，不出工具栏。
- **新手引导改在真实的岛上进行**：引导打开时先让出桌面，屏幕变暗，只留真实岛屿周围一圈亮光，卡片在它下面；打开岛屿、把文件拖上去、按剪贴板快捷键都在真的岛上练习。引导期间岛上放一首示例歌曲和一个示例 Agent 会话，开始和结束时岛会在消息面板里打招呼，结束后把之前的窗口恢复回来。
- **翻译页重新设计**：直接画在岛上，不再套卡片。方向可选自动 / 中→英 / 英→中，右侧显示服务和模型，小圆点表示密钥是否已保存，点它打开翻译设置。原文和译文两栏，岛够宽时左右并排、窄时上下排列；粘贴、清空、字数和「翻译 Enter」在原文下方，复制在译文下方。没有密钥或出错时在译文栏说明原因，并给出「打开设置」「重试」。
- **开合动画**：「弹性」展开和收起都和官网的岛一致，收起时文字不再左右抖；「Q 弹」改成弹出、回弹一次就停，不再来回晃好几下。
- **浅色主题**：浅色岛上大量看不清的文字修好了：错误、警告、提示和链接的颜色，设置里的说明文字，日志框，收起条上的 Agent 会话数，剪贴板弹窗和列表里的提示，Agent 卡片上的「正在运行」，待办里以后日子的标签；设置里开合动画的小预览也画成浅色的岛。
- **修复**：
  - 切换页面时只淡入，不再向上滑 6 像素，文字和图标不会在落定时跳一下。
  - 缩放为 100% 时，日历的日期在面板展开后不再挪动。
  - 新手引导的卡片不再挡住展开的岛。

## About this release

This is the second beta of 0.3.7. It reaches only users who chose the Beta channel under Settings → About → Version, or who install it by hand from the Releases page; users on the stable channel are not offered it. Beta users still get every final release, and switching back to Stable never downgrades. Install over your current version. Todos, notes, settings and connected mailboxes are kept.

## What's New

- **More from a screenshot**: copying puts a picture, a PNG and the file on the clipboard, so chat apps, Paint and terminals can all paste it; `Ctrl+S` or the toolbar saves to Pictures\Fuyu Screenshots, named by time; `T` pins the selection to the screen where it was taken: drag it, zoom with the wheel, fade it with `Ctrl` + wheel, and right-click to copy, save, reset the size, click through, hide all or close; `T` on a pin or the island hides or shows every pin. New shortcut `Ctrl+Shift+2` copies a region or window as soon as you pick it, without the toolbar.
- **The first-run guide happens on the real island**: it clears the desktop, dims the screen except for a lit ring around the island, and puts its card below it; opening the island, dropping a file on it and pressing the clipboard shortcut are practised on the real thing. While it runs the island plays a demo song and a demo Agent session, greets you in its message panel at the start and the end, and your windows come back afterwards.
- **Translation page redesigned**: drawn on the island itself, without a card. Pick Auto / ZH → EN / EN → ZH; the service and model sit on the right with a dot for whether the key is saved, and clicking them opens the translation settings. Source and result are two panes, side by side on a wide island and stacked on a narrow one; paste, clear, the character count and "Translate Enter" sit under the source, copy under the result. A missing key or an error is explained in the result pane, with "Open settings" and "Retry".
- **Open and close**: Spring now opens and closes like the website's island, and text no longer jitters while it closes; Bouncy springs out and back once and settles instead of wobbling.
- **Light theme**: much text that was hard to read on a light island is fixed: error, warning, info and link colours, settings hints, log boxes, the Agent count on the collapsed bar, hints in the clipboard popup and lists, the Agent card's "Running", and later days' todo tags; the open-and-close preview in settings draws a light island too.
- **Fixes**:
  - Switching pages only fades the new page in, without a 6px slide, so text and icons no longer jump as it lands.
  - At 100% scaling the calendar's dates no longer shift after the panel opens.
  - The first-run guide's card no longer covers the open island.
